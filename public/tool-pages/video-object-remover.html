Frequently Asked Questions

What video formats are supported?

We support MP4, MOV, and WEBM formats. Videos should be under 6 seconds for optimal processing.

How accurate is the object removal?

Our AI achieves professional-quality results in most cases. The Nano Banana model excels at understanding context and filling removed areas naturally.

Do I need to manually select objects?

No! Simply describe what you want to remove in natural language, like "remove the person in red" or "remove the car".

What is FILM interpolation?

FILM (Frame Interpolation for Large Motion) is Google's advanced algorithm that creates smooth transitions between edited frames, ensuring natural motion.

Is there a watermark on processed videos?

No, all videos are exported without watermarks at full quality.
